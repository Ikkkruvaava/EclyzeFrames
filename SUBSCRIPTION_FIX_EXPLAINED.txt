PROBLEM IDENTIFIED:
==================

Your yearly subscriptions show as "completed" immediately after first payment
because the Razorpay subscription was created with total_count = 1.

┌──────────────┬─────────────────┬──────────────┬─────────────────────────┐
│ Period       │ Old total_count │ Duration     │ Result                  │
├──────────────┼─────────────────┼──────────────┼─────────────────────────┤
│ Yearly       │ 1               │ 1 year       │ ❌ Completes after 1 pay│
│ Monthly      │ 12              │ 1 year       │ ❌ Completes after 1 yr  │
│ Weekly       │ 52              │ 1 year       │ ❌ Completes after 1 yr  │
│ Daily        │ 30              │ 1 month      │ ❌ Completes after 1 mo  │
└──────────────┴─────────────────┴──────────────┴─────────────────────────┘

=====================

Extended all subscriptions to 15 years by updating total_count values:

RAZORPAY SUBSCRIPTION LIFECYCLE:
=================================

  created → authenticated → active → [payments...] → completed
     │                                                     ↑
     │                                                     │
     └─────────────> cancelled (manual)                   │
                                                           │
                     halted ←─────────────────────────────┘
                   (payment failures)


WEBHOOK EVENTS NOW HANDLED:
============================

1. subscription.activated
   └─> Initial subscription setup complete

2. subscription.charged
   └─> Each recurring payment successful
   └─> Creates AutoDonation record

3. subscription.completed  ← NEW
   └─> All total_count cycles completed
   └─> Marks subscription as completed in DB

4. subscription.halted  ← NEW
   └─> Too many payment failures
   └─> Marks subscription as halted in DB

5. subscription.cancelled  ← NEW
   └─> User/admin cancelled subscription
   └─> Additional confirmation from Razorpay

6. payment.captured
   └─> One-time payment successful

7. payment.failed
   └─> Payment attempt failed

QUESTIONS?

Q: Will this affect existing subscriptions?
A: No, only new subscriptions created after this change.

Q: What happens to old yearly subscriptions?
A: They'll complete after 1 payment as before. Users can create new ones.

Q: Can we update existing subscriptions?
A: Not directly through your code. Would need Razorpay API call or support.

Q: What if I want 100 years instead of 15?
A: Just change the numbers in both files (within Razorpay's limits).

Q: Do I need to change anything in Razorpay dashboard?
A: No, just ensure webhooks are enabled for the new events.
